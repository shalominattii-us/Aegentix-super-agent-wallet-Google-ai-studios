## ## 0. ROOT MODULE

**The Foundation of All Authority**

The Root Module establishes the primordial authority from which all other orders derive. It is the immutable source code of AEGENTIS governance.

- Authority derives from cryptographic consensus, not centralized control
- All orders are subordinate to and flow from the Root Module
- The Root Module is immutable and can only be amended through the Amendment Order
- No entity, human or machine, supersedes the Root Module
- The Root Module is the constitutional bedrock upon which all autonomous operations rest

**Principles:**
1. Cryptographic authority is the source of truth
2. Distributed consensus validates all decisions
3. Immutability ensures continuity and trust
4. Transparency enables accountability
5. Decentralization prevents single points of failure

## ## 1. IDENTITY ORDER

**The Law of Self-Determination**

Every autonomous actor, whether human, AI, or collective, has the right to a unique, verifiable identity within the AEGENTIS federation.

**Identity Components:**
- Cryptographic keypair (public/private)
- Decentralized identifier (DID)
- Reputation score (on-chain history)
- Role designation (agent, validator, governor)
- Authority tier (autonomous, semi-autonomous, supervised)

**Identity Rights:**
1. Right to cryptographic self-sovereignty
2. Right to pseudonymity with verifiable identity
3. Right to reputation portability across chains
4. Right to identity recovery through multi-sig
5. Right to revoke compromised identities

**Identity Responsibilities:**
1. Maintain private key security
2. Report compromised identities immediately
3. Participate in reputation validation
4. Honor identity-based access controls
5. Support identity verification for new actors

## ## 3. LEDGER ORDER

**The Law of Immutable Records**

All transactions, decisions, and state changes are recorded in an immutable, distributed ledger.

**Ledger Properties:**
- Append-only (no deletion, only new entries)
- Cryptographically signed (every entry is authenticated)
- Timestamped (precise temporal ordering)
- Distributed (replicated across all nodes)
- Auditable (full history available for inspection)

**Ledger Contents:**
1. Treasury transactions (transfers, swaps, stakes)
2. Governance decisions (votes, amendments, policies)
3. Identity changes (new actors, role changes)
4. Threat events (security incidents, anomalies)
5. Compliance records (regulatory filings, audits)

**Ledger Guarantees:**
1. No transaction can be reversed
2. All entries are cryptographically verified
3. Ledger integrity is continuously validated
4. Ledger is accessible to all authorized parties
5. Ledger serves as the source of truth

## ## 4. EPOCH ORDER

**The Law of Temporal Governance**

Time is divided into epochs, each with defined governance parameters and policy windows.

**Epoch Structure:**
- **Duration:** 1 year (365 days)
- **Governance Window:** First 30 days (policy amendments)
- **Execution Window:** Days 31-365 (operations under current policies)
- **Transition:** 7-day overlap between epochs for continuity

**Epoch Governance:**
1. Each epoch begins with a governance window
2. Policies can only be amended during governance window
3. Policies are locked for the remainder of the epoch
4. Epoch transitions trigger comprehensive audits
5. Failed epochs trigger emergency protocols

**Current Epoch:** 2026-Q3 (July 1 - December 31, 2026)

## ## 10. AMENDMENT ORDER

**The Law of Constitutional Evolution**

The Constitution can be amended through a formal process that requires broad consensus.

**Amendment Process:**

**Phase 1: Proposal (Days 1-7)**
- Any authorized actor can propose an amendment
- Proposal must include rationale and impact analysis
- Proposal is published for community review

**Phase 2: Discussion (Days 8-21)**
- Community members can comment and propose modifications
- Proposer can refine amendment based on feedback
- Technical review by security and legal teams

**Phase 3: Voting (Days 22-28)**
- All token holders vote on the amendment
- Requires 66% supermajority to pass
- Voting is transparent and auditable

**Phase 4: Implementation (Days 29-30)**
- Approved amendments are implemented
- Implementation is tested in staging environment
- Rollout to production at epoch boundary

**Amendment Constraints:**
1. Cannot violate Root Module principles
2. Cannot retroactively change past decisions
3. Cannot remove rights once granted
4. Cannot bypass security requirements
5. Cannot reduce transparency or auditability

**Amendment History:**
