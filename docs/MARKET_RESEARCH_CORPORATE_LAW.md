## PART 2: CORPORATE LAW FRAMEWORK

### 2.1 Corporate Structure Options

#### Traditional Corporation (C-Corp)
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Liability** | Limited liability for shareholders |
| **Taxation** | Double taxation (corporate + shareholder) |
| **Governance** | Board of directors, shareholders |
| **Regulation** | State corporate law, SEC oversight |
| **Best For** | Large enterprises, public companies |

#### Limited Liability Company (LLC)
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Liability** | Limited liability for members |
| **Taxation** | Pass-through taxation (flexible) |
| **Governance** | Member-managed or manager-managed |
| **Regulation** | State LLC law, more flexible |
| **Best For** | Small to mid-size businesses, partnerships |

#### Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Liability** | Emerging legal framework (varies by jurisdiction) |
| **Governance** | Smart contracts, token-based voting |
| **Taxation** | Uncertain (evolving regulatory landscape) |
| **Regulation** | Minimal (regulatory gray area) |
| **Best For** | Web3, blockchain-native organizations |

#### Benefit Corporation (B-Corp)
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Liability** | Limited liability with social mission |
| **Taxation** | Standard corporate taxation |
| **Governance** | Board + stakeholder accountability |
| **Regulation** | State B-Corp law + certification |
| **Best For** | Mission-driven organizations with social impact |

### 2.4 Autonomous Agent Legal Framework

#### Legal Personhood for AI Agents
**Current Status:**
- No universal legal framework for AI personhood
- EU exploring "electronic personhood" for robots
- US treating AI as tools of their operators
- Blockchain-based smart contracts have limited legal recognition

**AEGENTIS Approach:**
- Agents operate under corporate legal entity
- Human operators retain ultimate liability
- Multi-sig approval gates autonomous authority
- Audit trails establish accountability chain

#### Liability & Insurance
| Risk | Coverage | Mechanism |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Operational Error** | E&O Insurance | Autonomous transaction errors |
| **Security Breach** | Cyber Insurance | Unauthorized access, data theft |
| **Regulatory Violation** | Compliance Insurance | Unintentional regulatory breach |
| **Smart Contract Bug** | Code Insurance | Logic errors in autonomous execution |

#### Contractual Framework
**Key Contracts:**
1. **Service Agreement** - Terms of autonomous treasury service
2. **Risk Disclosure** - Acknowledgment of autonomous operation risks
3. **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** - Uptime, performance guarantees
4. **Indemnification** - Liability allocation between parties
5. **Termination Clause** - Conditions for service discontinuation

### 2.5 Multi-Sig Authority & Governance

#### Tiered Authorization Framework
```
Transaction Amount | Required Signers | Approval Time | Audit Level
$0 - $1M          | 1 (Autonomous)   | Immediate     | Standard
$1M - $100M       | 2 (1 Human)      | 1 hour        | Enhanced
$100M - $1B       | 3 (2 Humans)     | 24 hours      | Critical
$1B+              | Board + Legal    | 5 days        | Full Review
```

#### Signer Qualifications
- **Autonomous Agent**: Verified identity, policy compliance, risk assessment
- **Human Signers**: Executive officers, board members, external auditors
- **Legal Review**: General counsel, compliance officer for large transactions

#### Dispute Resolution
1. **Automated Verification** - Smart contract validation
2. **Escalation Protocol** - Manual review for edge cases
3. **Arbitration** - Third-party dispute resolution
4. **Legal Action** - Court enforcement if necessary
